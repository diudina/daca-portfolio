### My contribution

I created the CV and LinkedIn assessment criteria for UrbanStyle's Data Analyst hiring guide.

The section focuses on what a hiring manager should look for during the first screening: clear role positioning, evidence behind technical skills, quantified business impact, relevant portfolio projects, and signs that a candidate can work independently in a fast-growing retail and e-commerce environment.

My contribution includes:

- CV assessment criteria
- LinkedIn and Featured-section criteria
- 3 green flags
- 2 red flags
- UrbanStyle-specific hiring considerations
- a main hiring principle: **evidence over claims**
