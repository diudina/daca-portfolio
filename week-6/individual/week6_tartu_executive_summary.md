# Tartu Store — Executive Summary

## Key findings

1. **Revenue grew 13% YoY to €260K in 2024.**
2. **Growth was volume-driven:** orders increased 16% and purchasing customers increased 18%.
3. **Average Order Value declined 3% to €287**, so higher spend per order did not drive the revenue increase.
4. **2024 outperformed 2023 in 9 of 12 months**, although monthly performance remained volatile.
5. **April and May showed the strongest short-term swing:** April revenue was 31% below 2023, followed by a 97% YoY increase in May.

## Business interpretation

Tartu is not a declining store in the analysed period. Its 2024 growth came mainly from more purchasing customers and more orders, while spend per order softened slightly.

## Recommendation

Maintain the higher customer and order volume while testing ways to improve basket value, such as cross-selling or product bundles. The large month-to-month swings should also be investigated with additional context such as promotions, stock availability, campaigns, or local events before assigning a cause.
