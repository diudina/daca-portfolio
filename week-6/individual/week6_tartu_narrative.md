# Tartu Store — Data Story

Tartu performed better in 2024 than the original scenario suggested, with revenue increasing **13% year over year to €260K**. Growth was mainly volume-driven: orders increased **16%** and purchasing customers increased **18%**, while average order value declined slightly by **3% to €287**. Monthly performance was uneven despite the positive full-year result, including a **31% YoY decline in April** followed by a **97% YoY increase in May**. The next opportunity is to maintain customer and order growth while testing ways to improve average order value.
