# Week 6 — Team Combined View

## Combined Story

UrbanStyle's locations show different but complementary performance patterns. **Tallinn remains the core revenue driver**, **Online is the fastest-growing channel**, **Tartu is growing through higher customer and order volume rather than higher spend per order**, and **Pärnu shows moderate seasonality rather than an extreme summer-only pattern**. Across the network, the strongest opportunities are to scale successful customer-retention and product strategies, understand the drivers behind strong peak periods, and improve weaker periods without assuming causes that are not supported by the data.

## Location Summary

- **Tallinn:** Core revenue driver — **€1.09M total revenue** across **3,801 orders**
- **Tartu:** Volume-driven growth — **€260K revenue in 2024, +13% YoY**
- **Pärnu:** Moderate seasonality — **€288.74K total revenue**, with August near **€33K**
- **Online:** Fastest growth — **€536.21K revenue in 2024, +37.9% YoY**

## Cross-Location Comparison

### Tallinn — strongest physical-store revenue base

Tallinn is UrbanStyle's flagship store and core revenue driver, generating **€1.09M in total revenue across 3,801 orders**. Monthly sales peaked in **December at €110,426.51**, rebounding sharply from an October baseline of **€82,561.84**.

Top performance is anchored by lead products such as **Praktiline džersii seelik (€10,540.86)** and supported by higher-tier loyalty members, particularly silver and gold customers.

**Recommendation:** export Tallinn's VIP loyalty-retention and product-bundling practices to secondary regional hubs where appropriate.

---

### Tartu — growth driven by customer and order volume

The task scenario suggested that Tartu had a declining trend, but the actual data does not support that. Tartu revenue increased from approximately **€229K in 2023 to €260K in 2024**, representing **13% YoY growth**.

The growth appears to be mainly volume-driven:
- Orders increased by **16%**
- Purchasing customers increased by **18%**
- Average Order Value decreased slightly by **3% to €287**

This means the store grew because more customers were purchasing and more orders were placed, rather than because customers were spending more per order.

The monthly comparison also shows that growth was not consistent throughout the year. **2024 outperformed 2023 in 9 of 12 months**, but there were large swings. For example, **April was 31% below 2023**, followed by a **97% YoY increase in May**.

The Top 5 products by revenue do not indicate strong dependence on a few individual products, so product mix was not used as the main explanation for Tartu's growth.

**Recommendation:** maintain customer and order-volume growth while looking for opportunities to improve average order value and investigating the reasons behind large monthly swings.

---

### Pärnu — moderate seasonality, not an extreme summer-only pattern

Pärnu is UrbanStyle's smallest physical store, generating **€288.74K from 747 customers**.

Sales show a summer lift, but the pattern is moderate rather than dramatic:
- **August peaks near €33K**
- Monthly average is approximately **€24K**
- **May is the softest month at about €19.5K**

The product mix also includes cooler-weather items among the top sellers, which suggests that Pärnu should not be treated as a purely tourist-season business.

**Recommendation:** use a light spring promotion to smooth the May dip rather than designing the entire store strategy around summer seasonality.

---

### Online Store — fastest-growing channel

Online revenue grew **37.9% in 2024**, rising from **€388.97K in 2023 to €536.21K in 2024**.

Across 2023–2024, the online channel generated:
- **€925.18K revenue**
- **3,191 orders**
- **€289.94 average order value**

**December was the strongest month at €63.2K**, and the top online product generated **€13.9K in revenue**.

**Recommendation:** identify the products and customer behaviour behind the December peak and test whether those successful patterns can be repeated in other periods.

## Team-Level Findings

1. **Tallinn remains UrbanStyle's strongest physical-store revenue base**, making it the most useful source of proven loyalty and product strategies.
2. **Online is the fastest-growing channel**, with 2024 revenue increasing **37.9% YoY**.
3. **Tartu is not declining:** revenue increased **13% YoY**, driven by more orders and purchasing customers rather than higher order value.
4. **Pärnu does show seasonality, but it is moderate**, so the data does not support treating it as a purely summer-driven store.
5. **Peak months differ by location and channel**, suggesting that local or channel-specific drivers should be investigated before applying one network-wide explanation.

## Recommendation for Anna

Use **Tallinn as the source of proven retention and product-bundling practices**, while treating the other locations according to their own performance patterns rather than applying a single strategy everywhere.

For **Tartu**, protect customer and order-volume growth while working on basket value. For **Pärnu**, focus on smoothing the spring dip rather than over-investing in a summer-only strategy. For **Online**, investigate the December peak and test whether the successful product and customer patterns can be replicated.

## Missing Data

The current sales data shows **what** happened, but it is not sufficient to explain every **why**. To make stronger recommendations, the team would benefit from additional context such as:

- Promotions and campaign timing
- Stock availability and stock-outs
- Customer retention and repeat-purchase behaviour
- Product-level margin data
- Store operating costs
- Customer satisfaction data
- Local events or competitor activity

These would help distinguish correlation from actual business drivers.
